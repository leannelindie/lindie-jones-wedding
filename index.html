TRAVEL INSURANCE

This is not to scare you, it is a real-world reminder of why travel insurance matters.

Time after time I have watched destination wedding guests lose every penny they paid toward a trip because they skipped insurance, thinking, I am going on this trip. The truth is, you never know what could happen.

I have personally seen clients cancel because:
• A close family member passed away
• Flights were disrupted by government shutdown and they could not get in or out
• They became seriously ill right before departure
• Unexpected events popped up that no one could have planned for

Hurricanes happen, natural disasters happen, jobs change, life happens. Once a trip reaches 100% penalty resorts will not bend, even with documentation. If insurance is not in place, they will not allow a date change or refund.

Please take a few minutes to review your options and ask yourself, am I willing to risk everything I have paid to avoid the cost of insurance today?

WILL I NEED A PASSPORT?

Yes! A passport is needed to fly to and from the U.S. and Cancun for all passengers. Please take the time now to locate your passport and check the expiration date; make sure it is valid for 6 months after your anticipated return date. Check this site for more passport info: travel.state.gov/content/passports/en/passports.html

I FOUND A CHEAPER PRICE ONLINE, CAN YOU MATCH IT?

Our rates come directly from the resort’s group department, ensuring that all guests are booked within the official wedding block. If you find a rate that seems significantly lower, keep in mind that sometimes “too good to be true” really is.

Many times, the resort is willing to price match, but they typically will not match rates from third-party sites that often come with booking issues, hidden fees, or restrictions. These sites may seem cheaper upfront, but they can lead to unexpected complications, such as missing reservations, lack of flexibility, and no access to wedding group perks.

The resort will never match a due in full, non-refundable, non-transferrable rate.

It’s important to note that if you book outside of the official block, the couple will face additional fees and penalties from the resort. To avoid this, please allow us the opportunity to try and match what you’ve found.
